Privacy Policy – HireOS Interview Zoom App

Effective date: [Date]

This policy explains what information the HireOS Interview app ("the App") collects from Zoom, how it is used and how it is protected. The App is operated by [Company legal name] ("we", "us"). It applies only to data the App receives through its Zoom integration.

1. Information we collect from Zoom

When an interviewer connects their Zoom account and uses the App, we collect:

  • Zoom account information: the Zoom user ID and display name of the connected account.
  • Authorization tokens: the OAuth access and refresh tokens that let the App act on the interviewer's behalf.
  • Meeting information: the ID, passcode and join link of the interview meetings the App creates.
  • Meeting transcripts: the live transcript of interview meetings created by the App, including each speaker's display name and what they said. Interviewers and candidates who take part in the meeting may appear in the transcript.

We do not record or store meeting audio or video, and we do not access meetings that the App did not create.

2. How we use the information

  • To create and manage interview meetings on the interviewer's Zoom account.
  • To let the interviewer host the meeting inside HireOS.
  • To provide a live transcript of the interview, and to let the hiring team review it.
  • To generate a draft interview summary and draft evaluation scores from the transcript, which a person reviews before use.

We do not sell this information and do not use it for advertising.

3. Sharing

  • OpenAI: transcript text and the interview's evaluation criteria are sent to the OpenAI API to generate the draft summary and scores. Zoom account tokens are never shared.
  • Your organization's hiring team: transcripts and results are visible to the users of your organization's HireOS workspace.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information when required by law.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on servers in Google Cloud [region]. Zoom authorization tokens and account information are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Meeting links and transcripts are stored in our database with access limited to the application.
